Frequently asked questions

How much does a private transfer cost?

It depends on the route and the time of day, and it is always a fixed price per car, not per person. Message us on WhatsApp with your pickup, destination, day and time, and we will give you the exact price in writing before you confirm. That is what you pay on arrival, with no extras and no surprises.

How do I book a driver? Can I book on WhatsApp?

Yes: yavoy bookings are made on WhatsApp only. Send us your pickup, destination, day, time and number of passengers. A real person replies with a fixed price in writing and, once you confirm, sends you the driver's name, the car and the plate. We reconfirm the day before.

Do I have to pay for the transfer in advance?

No. With yavoy you pay nothing when you book: you pay the driver when you are dropped off, in cash, by card or with Bizum. The price is the one we gave you in writing when you booked. If you need an invoice, we will issue one.

What is the difference between a VTC and a taxi in Málaga?

Price and booking. A taxi charges by the meter at the official rate, which is higher at night and on public holidays, and you can take one at a rank. A VTC like yavoy charges a fixed price agreed before the ride and can only work with a prior booking, so you know what you will pay before you get in.

Are you legal? Do you have a licence?

Yes. yavoy is a company with VTC licence no. 12011555 from the Junta de Andalucía for passenger transport with a driver. As the law requires, we only work with prior bookings: your WhatsApp confirmation is the contract. That is why we never pick up passengers on the street or at ranks without a booking.

What does the price include? Are there any extras?

Everything for the ride. The fixed price yavoy gives you on WhatsApp includes motorway tolls, night service and luggage, so there are no extras and no surprises: on arrival you pay exactly the amount we agreed, in cash, by card or with Bizum.

How many people fit in the car?

Up to 4 passengers per car, with their luggage. If there are more of you, or you are bringing golf bags or a lot of luggage, tell us when you book on WhatsApp and we will suggest the best option for your group, always with a fixed price before you confirm.

Which rides do you do?

Any ride inside or outside the province of Málaga: the airport, the port and María Zambrano station, dinners and nights out, golf courses, weddings, day trips to Ronda, Granada, Seville or Gibraltar, and hourly driver hire. yavoy is based in Mijas and drives all along the Costa del Sol, always with a fixed price before we set off.

Do you do rides within the same town, or only between towns?

Both. yavoy does rides within the same town, such as from your home to a restaurant in Marbella or from Málaga city centre to the airport, and between towns all along the Costa del Sol, such as Mijas to Marbella or La Cala to Fuengirola. Always with a fixed price per car before we set off.

Can I cancel a transfer?

Yes, whenever you need to. A WhatsApp message is enough; the sooner you tell us, the sooner we can free up the car for someone else. Since you pay nothing in advance with yavoy, there is nothing to refund and no paperwork.
